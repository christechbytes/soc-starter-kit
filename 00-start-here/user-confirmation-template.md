# User Confirmation Message Template

Sometimes the fastest way to classify an alert is to ask the person involved (if that is allowed): "Was this you?" How you ask matters.

## Before you reach out

- **Be neutral, not accusatory.** Most alerts turn out to be legitimate. You're confirming, not interrogating
- **Use a trusted channel.** If the account might be compromised, an attacker could be reading its email or chat. Use a known phone number, a separate channel, or go through their manager
- **Share only what's needed.** Give enough detail for them to recognize the activity. Don't share alert names, detection logic, or other users' information
- **Never include links or ask for passwords.** Your message shouldn't look like phishing
- **Follow your company's process.** Some teams require going through the manager or HR first

## Template: chat or email

**Subject:** Quick security check: activity on your account

Hi [Name],

This is [Your name] from the security team. We noticed some activity on your account and want to confirm it was you. This is a routine check.

**Activity:** [What happened, in plain language]
**When:** [Date and time, with time zone]
**From:** [Location or device, if helpful]

Could you reply to confirm whether this was you? If it wasn't, or you're not sure, please let me know right away and don't make any changes to your account until we've spoken.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Team and contact details]

## Template: phone call

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from the security team. I'm doing a routine check on some recent activity on your account. Do you have a minute?
>
> On [date] at around [time], your account [what happened in plain language]. Was that you?"

**If yes:** ask what it was for, so you can document the reason.
**If no or unsure:** thank them, tell them the team will follow up, and escalate right away.

## Template: no response follow up

Hi [Name],

Following up on my message from [time] about activity on your account. Could you confirm whether this was you when you get a moment? If I don't hear back by [time], I'll check in with [manager's name] to help confirm.

Thanks,
[Your name]

## Example (fictional)

**Subject:** Quick security check: activity on your account

Hi Demo User,

This is Demo Analyst from the security team. We noticed some activity on your account and want to confirm it was you. This is a routine check.

**Activity:** Your account viewed and downloaded files from a cloud storage location used for customer reports
**When:** Feb 3, between 22:41 and 23:40 UTC
**From:** A location we don't usually see for your account

Could you reply to confirm whether this was you? If it wasn't, or you're not sure, please let me know right away and don't make any changes to your account until we've spoken.

Thanks,
Demo Analyst
Security Operations

## What to record

Whatever the answer, note it in your [Investigation Notes](investigation-notes-template.md):

- Who you contacted, how, and when
- What they said, in their words if possible
- If no response, when you followed up and who you escalated to
